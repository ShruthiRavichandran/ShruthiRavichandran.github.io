Shruthi Ravichandran

Hi! I'm Shruthi, a second-year PhD student at UC Berkeley, where I work with Professors Miki Lustig in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science and Sandya Subramanian in Computational Precision Health. I received my S.B. in EECS and B.S.A.D. in Art and Design from MIT in 2025, where I worked with Professor Loza Tadesse and Professor Giovanni Traverso at MIT MechE. In my free time, you can find me in nature, making music, and building things! Click through the tabs to see some projects I've worked on and please feel free to contact me at shruthi (at) berkeley (dot) edu!

Research

I'm passionate about using engineering to create healthcare solutions that are accessible to all. I currently work with Profs. Miki Lustig and Sandya Subramanian at UC Berkeley. In the past, I have been fortunate to work with Profs. Loza Tadesse and Giovanni Traverso at MIT, Prof. Stathis Karathanasis at Case Western Reserve University, and Prof. Jean-Pierre Raskin at UCLouvain.
